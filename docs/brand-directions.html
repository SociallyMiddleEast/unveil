Four ways to open a wedding invitation

Each direction is built around one signature moment, something the guest does to open the invitation. This comes from the video you shared: the gesture is what makes people say “this is an invitation?”

Tap each phone to try the moment. Every invite then leads to the same product: details, RSVP, location and a countdown.

Direction 1

Unveil.

Every invitation deserves an entrance.

Opening the invitation is staged like a moment at the wedding itself. The guest lifts a sheer bridal veil to reveal the couple’s names. It is dramatic, cinematic and made for screen recordings, closest in spirit to the video you liked but built around the veil instead of glitter and curtains.

Direction 2

Yasmineinvitations

Invitations that bloom.

Jasmine climbs the walls of old Lebanese houses and is part of almost every summer wedding here. The name feels local and warm, and it is still easy to say in English for the diaspora. The guest taps the screen and a drift of jasmine petals scatters to reveal the invitation. Fresh, romantic, and very shareable.

Direction 3

Zaffa

The celebration starts the moment they open it.

Named after the zaffe, the drums and dancing that open every Lebanese wedding. This is the most energetic direction. The guest taps a drum, it beats, sound and confetti burst out, and the invitation appears. It is proudly Lebanese, with the confidence of a modern brand.

Signature moment

Tap the drum. With sound on, a short darbouka roll plays, and guests can pick the zaffe song for their arrival.

Voice

Joyful and loud, with a wink. “Clear your calendar and warm up your dabke.”

Best forCouples who want fun over formality, plus engagement parties, birthdays and other events.
Watch outVery tied to weddings. Check it still feels right for corporate events if those become important.

Direction 4

Cachet

Sealed with intention.

Cachet is French for a wax seal and also means prestige, a double meaning that lands naturally in a francophone-friendly market like Lebanon. The guest breaks a wax seal, the envelope opens, and a clean, couture-style card slides out. This is the most refined, minimal direction: luxury that whispers instead of shouting.

What happens next

Pick one, or mix them

  1. Choose the direction that feels most like you. You can combine them, for example the Cachet name with the Unveil palette.
  2. Check the name is free: Instagram and TikTok handle, a .com or .com.lb domain, and a trademark search with Lebanon’s Ministry of Economy and Trade.
  3. Design the logo and a full brand kit: logo, colors, fonts, icons and social templates.
  4. Build one real, working sample invitation to use in your first reaction-style videos.

The names, colors and moments here are first drafts. The phone demos are simplified previews, not the finished product.
