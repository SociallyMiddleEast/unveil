01 · Logo

A Lebanese arch, a parted veil

The mark is built from the triple-arched window of the traditional Lebanese house. Inside the tall centre arch, a veil parts in champagne gold: the moment every Unveil invitation is built around. It is local without being a cliché, and it works from a business card down to an app icon.

1

The triple arch. The silhouette of old Beirut and mountain homes, with their red roofs and three tall windows. It says “home” and “Lebanon” in one shape.

2

The parted veil. Two gold lines open from a single point, like a veil lifted or curtains drawn back. This is the brand promise.

3

The threshold. The base line is the doorstep: the guest is invited in.

4

The gold dot. In the wordmark, the full stop says “this is the moment.” It is the only upright character in an italic word.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo at least as wide as the centre arch.

Smallest size

Mark alone: 20 px on screen, 8 mm in print. Wordmark: 80 px wide. Below that, use the mark only.

Never

Don't stretch, outline or add shadows, set the wordmark upright, or put it on a busy photo without a wine overlay.

04 · Voice

Speak like the moment before the doors open

Unveil sounds like a gracious host: warm, assured and never loud. Short sentences, a little mystery, and the couple always comes first.

Anticipatory

We build suspense and let the reveal do the talking. Hint, don’t explain.

Gracious

Warm, generous and polite, the way Lebanese hospitality feels. Guests are honoured, not processed.

Assured

Confident without bragging. No exclamation marks, no “amazing!!”, no discount-shop energy.

We say

  • Something beautiful is waiting.
  • Lift gently.
  • Your story, revealed.
  • We’ll keep a seat warm for you.

We don’t say

  • Click here to view your e-card!!
  • Cheap, fast digital invites
  • Download our template now
  • OMG you won’t believe this

05 · The reveal collection

Every invitation opens with a gesture

These are the signature openings the product is built around. Each is named so couples can choose one like a dress style. The Veil is the flagship. Every reveal leads to the same invitation: details, RSVP, location, schedule, gift list and a countdown.

The Veil Flagship
Guest lifts a sheer veil

Soft, bridal and intimate. The brand’s own story.

The Arches
Guest opens three wooden window shutters

Lebanese heritage-house shutters swing open on to the sea or the mountains.

The Seal
Guest breaks a wax seal

A gold-wax monogram cracks and the envelope opens. Classic and formal.

The Petals
Guest blows on the screen

Jasmine or rose petals drift away. Made for garden and summer weddings.

The Ballroom
Guest taps and the lights come up

A dark room glows, the chandelier lights, and the names appear in gold.

The Zaffe
Guest taps a drum

A darbouka roll with sound, then the invitation dances in. Pure joy.

07 · Securing the name

“Unveil” is a common word, so we claim it with a descriptor

A quick search found no invitation company named Unveil, but other wedding brands use “unveil” in their marketing. The bare word will be hard to trademark or get as a handle, so the full name always carries “Invitations”. All of the handles and domains below are still unchecked suggestions.
