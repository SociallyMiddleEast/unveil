Questions

Good to know.

How do guests receive it?

As a link, usually on WhatsApp. Each family gets their own link that greets them by name and knows how many seats are saved for them.

Do guests need to download anything?

No. It opens in the browser on any phone or computer.

How do guests reply?

Right inside the invitation, in a few seconds. Their answer goes straight to your guest list, and they can change it later.

Can we change details after it’s sent?

Yes. If the time or venue changes, we update it and every guest sees the new details the next time they open their link.

What does a guest count include?

Every person invited. A family of four counts as four guests.

Is it only for weddings?

No. Engagements, kateb kteb, birthdays and baptisms work the same way.
